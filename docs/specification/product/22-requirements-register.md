# Requirements register

Status: authoritative extracted specification; target behavior, not implementation evidence.
Source: [HB-TRACE-V1-PLAN.md — Requirements register](../../../HB-TRACE-V1-PLAN.md#requirements-register)

<a id="prd-22-01"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-01 -->
Convert the supplied standards and certification document into controlled requirements. Each entry records:
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-01 -->

<a id="prd-22-02"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-02 -->
- source, edition and clause/reference;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-02 -->

<a id="prd-22-03"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-03 -->
- applicability and rationale;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-03 -->

<a id="prd-22-04"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-04 -->
- concise operational requirement;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-04 -->

<a id="prd-22-05"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-05 -->
- control owner and frequency;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-05 -->

<a id="prd-22-06"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-06 -->
- required evidence and linked form/SOP;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-06 -->

<a id="prd-22-07"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-07 -->
- measurable limit or acceptance rule where applicable;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-07 -->

<a id="prd-22-08"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-08 -->
- effective/review date, status and reviewer; and
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-08 -->

<a id="prd-22-09"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-09 -->
- superseding source when the standard changes.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-09 -->

<a id="prd-22-15"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-22-15 -->
The active versioned honey quality-rule set includes the reviewed honey limits, including moisture at no more than
20% m/m under the supplied honey standard, together with source edition, test method, unit and effective date.
All remaining composition, contaminant and microbiological requirements must be transcribed and independently
checked by the quality manager before release decisions depend on them. Licensed standards are not placed in
public assets or redistributed beyond their licence.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-22-15 -->
