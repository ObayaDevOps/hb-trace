# V1 outcomes

Status: authoritative extracted specification; target behavior, not implementation evidence.
Source: [HB-TRACE-V1-PLAN.md — V1 outcomes](../../../HB-TRACE-V1-PLAN.md#v1-outcomes)

<a id="prd-04-01"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-01 -->
V1 succeeds when Humble Beeing can:
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-01 -->

<a id="prd-04-02"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-02 -->
- install the staff PWA on supported phones, reopen permitted field work without a connection, and reconnect
  without losing or duplicating records;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-02 -->

<a id="prd-04-03"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-03 -->
- register staff, farmers, apiaries and collection locations;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-03 -->

<a id="prd-04-04"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-04 -->
- import and validate existing farmer/apiary records in bulk without creating duplicates;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-04 -->

<a id="prd-04-05"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-05 -->
- record honeycomb purchases or harvests and assign permanent internal identities;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-05 -->

<a id="prd-04-06"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-06 -->
- issue a farmer purchase receipt and record confirmation or a later dispute;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-06 -->

<a id="prd-04-07"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-07 -->
- record separation of honey and beeswax with complete source lineage;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-07 -->

<a id="prd-04-08"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-08 -->
- identify and track buckets, batches and packaging runs with durable QR labels;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-08 -->

<a id="prd-04-09"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-09 -->
- record processing, blending, quantities, moisture and basic quality decisions;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-09 -->

<a id="prd-04-10"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-10 -->
- trace backward from a sale batch to its sources and forward from a source to derived batches;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-10 -->

<a id="prd-04-11"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-11 -->
- operate from a practical dashboard showing stock, activity, holds, missing evidence and failed synchronization;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-11 -->

<a id="prd-04-12"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-12 -->
- publish a status-first, privacy-safe public batch page whose claims remain linked to approved evidence;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-12 -->

<a id="prd-04-13"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-13 -->
- print controlled production forms and upload photographs or scans of completed forms;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-13 -->

<a id="prd-04-14"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-14 -->
- give staff access to current approved SOPs and preserve historical versions;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-14 -->

<a id="prd-04-15"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-15 -->
- give non-technical users, developers and approved AI assistants separate, current paths through documentation
  that explains the product and codebase at the level each audience needs;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-15 -->

<a id="prd-04-16"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-16 -->
- retrieve a complete evidence pack by batch, date or record type;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-16 -->

<a id="prd-04-17"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-17 -->
- continue the explicitly supported collection workflows during poor connectivity and sync safely when
  connectivity returns;
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-17 -->

<a id="prd-04-18"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-18 -->
- conduct a batch-level hold, withdrawal and recall drill, including downstream and public-page impact; and
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-18 -->

<a id="prd-04-19"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-04-19 -->
- export the database and asset manifest in provider-neutral formats ready for a later infrastructure migration.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-04-19 -->
