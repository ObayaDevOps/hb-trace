# Included in V1

Status: authoritative extracted specification; target behavior, not implementation evidence.
Source: [HB-TRACE-V1-PLAN.md — Included in V1](../../../HB-TRACE-V1-PLAN.md#included-in-v1)

<a id="prd-06-01"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-01 -->
- Installable staff PWA, standalone navigation, restricted offline field operation and tested update/recovery
  behavior. Installation is optional for ordinary browser access and public batch lookup.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-01 -->

<a id="prd-06-02"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-02 -->
- Staff authentication and the roles defined in this plan.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-02 -->

<a id="prd-06-03"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-03 -->
- Farmers, apiaries, regions, collection locations and consent records.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-03 -->

<a id="prd-06-04"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-04 -->
- Validated fixed-template CSV/XLSX import for existing farmers and apiaries, including preview, error reporting
  and duplicate resolution before commit. A general-purpose column-mapping product is not part of V1.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-04 -->

<a id="prd-06-05"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-05 -->
- Honeycomb purchase/harvest records, including supplier, source, date, grade, quantity and payment reference,
  together with farmer receipts and a confirmation/dispute path.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-05 -->

<a id="prd-06-06"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-06 -->
- Honey and raw-beeswax separation, bucket creation, labels, storage and custody.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-06 -->

<a id="prd-06-07"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-07 -->
- Partial allocation, processing and blending with exact source contributions.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-07 -->

<a id="prd-06-08"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-08 -->
- Moisture measurements, laboratory-report attachments and authorised hold/release decisions.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-08 -->

<a id="prd-06-09"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-09 -->
- Honey packaging runs, lot/batch identifiers and batch-level public QR verification.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-09 -->

<a id="prd-06-10"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-10 -->
- A deliberately small, fixed public-claim register, privacy-safe actor profiles and approved story content kept
  separate from immutable traceability facts. V1 does not include a general claim-methodology engine or a rich
  editorial workflow product.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-10 -->

<a id="prd-06-11"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-11 -->
- An operations dashboard covering available stock, recent activity, holds, missing evidence, pending reviews
  and synchronization failures.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-11 -->

<a id="prd-06-12"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-12 -->
- Printable forms, completed-form evidence capture and supervisor review.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-12 -->

<a id="prd-06-13"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-13 -->
- A minimal controlled SOP and HACCP library covering metadata, versions, approval, effective status and
  retrieval; configurable document workflows and training automation are deferred.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-13 -->

<a id="prd-06-14"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-14 -->
- Repository-owned documentation for staff, developers and approved AI assistants. Building a separate searchable
  documentation product is not a V1 release dependency.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-14 -->

<a id="prd-06-15"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-15 -->
- A versioned UNBS requirements register for the supplied honey, hygiene, labelling and certification sources.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-15 -->

<a id="prd-06-16"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-16 -->
- Audit history, exports, managed-service backup/restore and non-container managed deployment.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-16 -->

<a id="prd-06-17"></a>
<!-- requirement:PRD-06-17 -->
- Batch-level hold, withdrawal and recall, including downstream impact and public publication withdrawal.
<!-- /requirement:PRD-06-17 -->
