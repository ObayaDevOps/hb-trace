# The experience

Status: authoritative extracted specification; target behavior, not implementation evidence.
Source: [docs/mobile-first-workflow-ui-plan.md — The experience](../../mobile-first-workflow-ui-plan.md#the-experience)

<a id="wf-02-01"></a>
<!-- requirement:WF-02-01 -->
A field officer signs in, registers or finds a farmer and apiary, then records
honey collections. Each physical bucket gets a readable code. HQ records its
arrival immediately, resolves its source, checks quality, processes the honey
and bottles it. The batch/lot code on a jar leads customers to the approved
story of the contributing apiaries and farmers.
<!-- /requirement:WF-02-01 -->

<a id="wf-02-02"></a>
<!-- requirement:WF-02-02 -->
Every screen answers **Where am I? What do I need to do? What happens next?**
Show one main action per step. Use separate field, receiving, production and
quality tasks, with only the actions appropriate to the signed-in user's role.
The system manages dependencies and traceability; staff follow short steps.
<!-- /requirement:WF-02-02 -->
