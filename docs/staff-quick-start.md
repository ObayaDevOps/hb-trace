# Staff quick start

This guide describes the local V1 surface. It is not a controlled SOP and does
not replace quality or production procedures.

1. Open **Sourcing & purchases** to stage a honeycomb purchase and receipt.
   A local save is labelled **pending locally** and is not a server-confirmed
   receipt.
2. To onboard records, open **Farmers** or **Apiaries** under **Source register**.
   Upload the fixed CSV/XLSX template, inspect the dry-run row errors, and
   resolve every likely duplicate with an explicit match, skip or create
   decision before committing. The original source is private local evidence;
   a completed local import is not hosted backup.
3. Use **Material & lineage** to review direct source contributions and follow
   a lot backward or forward. A batch/lot code is not an individual jar ID.
4. Use **Quality & holds** to stage measurements and keep suspect material
   contained. Release, hold, reject and recall decisions require an authorized
   online server action.
5. Use **Packaging & publication** to stage a packaging run. Publication is a
   separate approved snapshot and does not happen automatically from a quality
   measurement or uploaded document.
6. Use **Operations & compliance** to retrieve controlled form/SOP metadata and
   stage evidence metadata. The signed paper remains the authoritative floor
   record until the hosted evidence workflow is enabled.

When hosted mode is enabled, distinguish **pending locally**, **sending**,
**dependency pending**, **rejected**, **conflicted** and **safely acknowledged
by server**. Never clear an unsynced record as if it were backed up.
